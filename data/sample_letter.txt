Re: Contract Review - Software Licensing Agreement

Dear Ms. Davis,

I hope this letter finds you well. I am writing to provide you with our legal review of the software licensing agreement dated March 15, 2024, between Innovative Tech Corp and CloudSoft Solutions.

SUMMARY OF FINDINGS:
After careful review of the proposed agreement, we have identified several key areas that require attention before execution:

1. Liability Limitations: The current limitation clause caps damages at $50,000, which may be insufficient given the scope of services.

2. Intellectual Property Rights: Section 4.2 requires clarification regarding derivative works and customizations.

3. Termination Clauses: The 30-day termination notice may create operational challenges during critical periods.

4. Data Protection: Enhanced provisions needed for GDPR and CCPA compliance.

RECOMMENDATIONS:
1. Negotiate liability cap increase to $250,000
2. Add specific IP ownership language
3. Extend termination notice to 90 days
4. Include comprehensive data protection addendum

We estimate this review and negotiation process will require 15-20 hours of legal work at our standard rate of $450 per hour.

Please contact me at your earliest convenience to discuss these findings and coordinate next steps with CloudSoft Solutions' legal team.
